Kairui Liu

Geometric group theory and random walks

I am a mathematician working at the intersection of geometric group theory and probability. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the Beijing International Center for Mathematical Research at Peking University in 2026, under the supervision of Wenyuan Yang.

My research focuses on random walks on groups exhibiting hyperbolic features, especially the Martin boundary and its relationship to geometric boundaries. I use methods from the geometry of metric spaces, CAT(0) cube complexes, and the study of contracting and Morse geodesics.

Research

How does the geometry of a group help describe the long-term behavior of a random walk? I study this question through Ancona inequalities and the connections between Martin boundaries and geometric boundaries.
